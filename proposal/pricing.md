# Pricing

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Meridian Components Inventory Dashboard Modernization**

---

Two pricing structures are presented below. We are prepared to discuss either; our recommendation is noted.

---

## Option A — Fixed Fee by Phase *(Recommended)*

| Phase | Scope | Fixed Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Onboarding & architecture documentation (R4) | $9,500 |
| 2 | Reports module remediation (R1) | $18,000 |
| 3 | Automated browser testing (R3) | $11,500 |
| 4 | Restocking feature (R2) | $22,000 |
| **Total — Required Scope** | | **$61,000** |

**Optional extensions (D1–D3):**

| Item | Scope | Fixed Fee |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | UI modernisation | $9,000 |
| D2 | Internationalization extension | $6,500 |
| D3 | Dark mode | $4,500 |
| **Total — All Optional Items** | | **$20,000** |

**Total (required + all optional):** $81,000

Fixed-fee pricing provides Meridian with full cost predictability. Scope changes or additions identified during delivery are subject to a written change order process prior to work commencing.

---

## Option B — Time & Materials with Not-to-Exceed

**Rate:** $195/hour (blended, senior consultant level)
**Not-to-exceed — Required scope:** $68,000
**Not-to-exceed — All optional items:** $22,000
**Not-to-exceed — Full engagement:** $90,000

T&M provides flexibility if the scope of R1 remediation or R2 expands following the Phase 1 audit. Meridian is billed only for hours worked; unused budget within the NTE is not charged.

---

## Our Recommendation

Fixed fee (Option A) is recommended for this engagement. The scope of R2–R4 is well-defined; R1 carries some discovery risk, but the Phase 1 audit and defect register checkpoint are designed to surface that risk before remediation spend is committed. Fixed-fee pricing gives Meridian's procurement and finance teams a predictable number to plan against.

---

*All fees are in USD. Payment terms and invoicing schedule to be agreed at contract execution. Travel is not anticipated for this engagement; if required, travel costs are billed at cost with prior approval.*
