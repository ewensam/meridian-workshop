# Executive Summary

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Meridian Components Inventory Dashboard Modernization**
**Submitted by:** Accenture
**Date:** April 28, 2026

---

Meridian Components has a working dashboard and a capable team — what's missing is confidence. Your operations staff can't rely on the Reports module, your IT team won't approve changes without test coverage, and your Tokyo warehouse runs in a system that wasn't built for them. The previous vendor left the work unfinished. Our engagement is structured to close that gap.

Our proposed approach is direct: address what's broken first (R1), deliver the Restocking capability your operations team has been waiting for (R2), then establish the automated test coverage that positions Meridian IT to approve future changes without friction (R3). Architecture documentation (R4) is scoped as part of onboarding — our approach includes an independent codebase review rather than reliance on the previous vendor's limited handoff notes.

This proposal outlines a phased engagement structured around Meridian's stated priorities. Each phase is designed to close with a working deliverable, so value is not deferred to the end. The pricing section presents both a fixed-fee and a T&M structure; we are prepared to discuss whichever model better fits Meridian's preferences.

A few assumptions underpin this proposal, derived from the RFP and background materials:

- The Restocking view is scoped to current stock levels and an operator-supplied budget ceiling; demand forecasting is treated as a future phase.
- Test scope for R3 is intended to be agreed with Meridian IT prior to development. We propose a test plan; IT approves what's in scope.
- The desired items (D1–D3) are priced as optional extensions and are not intended to affect the required scope timeline.

Meridian's operations team depends on this system daily. We take that seriously.
